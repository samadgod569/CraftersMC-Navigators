Common questions
Make sure your start file and runtime are set correctly in the app settings. Check the logs tab for any error output — it usually tells you exactly what went wrong.
Suspended apps usually hit a resource limit or violated usage policies. Check the suspension reason in your dashboard and reach out on Discord if you think it was a mistake.
Go to the Backups section in your app panel, choose a backup and click Restore. You'll need to confirm your runtime and start file before the restore runs.
Premium upgrades are handled through our Discord server. Ping a developer or check the #billing channel for current pricing and availability.
