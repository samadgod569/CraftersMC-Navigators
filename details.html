Our mission

We believe AI infrastructure should be transparent, blazing fast, and truly developer-first. NyxoAI was born to eliminate the hassle of juggling multiple API keys, inconsistent billing, and hidden data policies. We give you one key, one dashboard, and access to every frontier model you actually need.

Founded by engineers who got tired of vendor lock-in, we built NyxoAI to be the neutral, open core for generative AI. Join thousands of developers who ship faster with Nyxo.

🔐 Your data stays yours

At NyxoAI, privacy isn’t an afterthought. We do not log, store, or train on any request payload. All data transmitted is ephemeral and strictly used to serve your inference. We never sell user data or share identifiable information with third parties.

📦 What we collect

We collect only essential account information (email, billing details) and aggregated usage metrics for system health. No prompts, completions, or API request contents are persisted beyond delivery.

🔒 Security & compliance

All connections are TLS 1.3 encrypted. API keys are hashed at rest. We comply with GDPR & CCPA — you can request data deletion anytime from your dashboard.
